Why so small, historically?
Pre-IRA rates ($10–50/ton) rarely penciled; nearly all volume went to EOR, not dedicated storage.
What changes after 2024?
IRA rates ($60–85/ton, $180/ton DAC) plus ExxonMobil's contracted pipeline and Tallgrass hub drive the step-change.
Uncertainty is substantial
270+ projects announced, 19 operating; Class VI permitting runs 2–4 years per well.

Structural gap
30% of capital vs. $85 per ton
Solar ITC = 30% of capital across hundreds of billions installed. 45Q = $85/ton across 12 MMmt. The math is inexorable; you cannot close the gap with a higher rate alone.
OBBBA cliff (2028–29)
Solar and wind fall off the table
The One Big Beautiful Bill Act compressed solar §48E and wind §45Y eligibility to placed-in-service by 12/31/2027, creating a sharp drop while 45Q was preserved through the decade.
Relative convergence
25% share by 2029, not by scaling up
45Q's share of the combined credit pool is projected to rise from 2.5% to ~25% by 2029, not because CCS scales dramatically, but because solar and wind fall off a cliff.
Regulatory note
Subpart RR uncertainty: EPA proposed eliminating Subpart RR (the MRV backbone for 45Q) in September 2024. IRS Notice 2026-1 issued a temporary safe harbor for 2025 only. The regulatory outlook for dedicated storage monitoring remains unsettled.
